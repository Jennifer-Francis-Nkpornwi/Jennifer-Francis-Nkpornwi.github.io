About Me

Business Analyst & Data Analyst Driving Better Business Decisions

I help organizations transform business challenges into practical, data-driven solutions through business analysis, process improvement, stakeholder collaboration, and analytics. My background in customer operations and sales enables me to bridge the gap between business needs and technology while delivering measurable outcomes.

Professional Profile

My career has been built around solving problems, improving customer experiences, and helping teams make informed decisions. Beginning in customer-facing and commercial roles, I developed a deep understanding of business operations, stakeholder communication, and process efficiency.

That foundation naturally evolved into Business Analysis and Data Analytics, where I now combine structured analysis, business process modelling, requirements gathering, and data storytelling to help organizations improve performance and deliver value.

I enjoy working on projects that require analytical thinking, collaboration across teams, and translating complex information into actionable business recommendations.
