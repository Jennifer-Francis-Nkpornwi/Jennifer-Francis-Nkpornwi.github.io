About

Clarity is the whole job.

I simplify complexity, ask the questions no one else wants to, and hand decision-makers something they can actually act on — that's the thread running through every role I've had.

Port Harcourt, Nigeria

I lived the problem. Now I solve it with data and processes.

I spent seven years inside real business operations — sales administration, customer service, business development — at Essilor Luxottica and Medicano Resources Limited. I wasn't watching business problems from the outside; I was accountable for them. Targets, escalations, process bottlenecks, the gap between what leadership assumed was happening and what was actually happening on the ground.

That experience is exactly why I moved into Business Analysis and Data Analytics — not to leave commercial work behind, but to get better tools for the same job: turning ambiguity into a decision someone can act on. I completed a full data analytics bootcamp and I'm currently a Data Analyst intern at MicroTech Africa, where I've delivered multiple end-to-end analysis projects from raw data to executive-ready dashboard.

My working toolkit is Excel, SQL, and Power BI on the data side, and Visio, Balsamiq, Jira, and Confluence on the business analysis side — the stack most businesses actually run on. I'm not interested in over-claiming tools I haven't shipped real work in; what's on this site is what I can defend in an interview, line by line.
