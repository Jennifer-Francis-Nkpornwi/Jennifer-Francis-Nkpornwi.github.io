Women's Clothing E-Commerce Review Analysis

Turning 23,486 customer reviews into a Power BI dashboard and an executive summary that show what customers like, what frustrates them, and where to improve first.

RoleData Analyst
ToolsPower BI, Excel, Power Query, Text Analytics
TypePersonal project
SourceView on GitHub ↗

The business question

The company's women's clothing e-commerce platform had 23,486 customer reviews and no structured read on what they were saying. The analysis set out to answer three questions:

  • How satisfied are customers, and how likely are they to recommend the products?
  • What influences whether a customer recommends a product?
  • Where are the opportunities to improve product quality and the overall customer experience?

My approach

I prepared the review data, analysed ratings, recommendation behaviour, product categories, sentiment, and customer feedback, then built a Power BI dashboard that can be filtered by division, department, product class, age group, and recommendation status. The findings and recommendations are written up in an executive summary.

  • Measured satisfaction through average rating and recommendation rate
  • Tested how closely customer ratings track recommendation behaviour
  • Compared satisfaction across departments and product classes
  • Judged product performance by Negative Review Rate (negative reviews as a share of each class's reviews) instead of raw complaint counts
  • Analysed review text for recurring themes in lower-rated reviews

What the analysis found

Customer satisfaction is strong: an average rating of 4.2 out of 5 and a recommendation rate of about 82%. Higher ratings were strongly linked to customers recommending the products, so improving satisfaction feeds directly into customer advocacy.

  • Bottoms, Intimate, and Jackets earned the highest average ratings, while Trend scored lowest, which makes it a clear target for improvement
  • Dresses (6,319 reviews), Knits (4,843), and Blouses (3,097) drew the most engagement, making them the most influential product classes
  • Lower-rated reviews kept returning to fit, sizing consistency, fabric quality, comfort, and overall appearance

Counts versus rates

Those same three classes also had the most negative reviews, but that mostly reflects how many reviews they receive. Negative Review Rate gives a fairer comparison: Blouses were highest at 11.2%, followed by Dresses at 10.9% and Knits at 10.4%. Tracking the rate of complaints rather than the count is a more accurate measure of how a product is really performing.

Recommendations

  • Prioritise quality improvements for Dresses, Knits, and Blouses, focusing on fit accuracy, sizing consistency, fabric quality, and product descriptions. These high-volume classes are where improvement is likely to cut negative reviews the most.
  • Set up continuous feedback monitoring that tracks ratings, recommendation behaviour, sentiment, and Negative Review Rate by product class, so emerging product issues are caught early.
